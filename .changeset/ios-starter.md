Create a new iOS or macOS app with `clerk init --starter`: pick iOS (SwiftUI) or macOS (SwiftUI) on a Mac with Xcode, and init writes a SwiftUI Xcode project, then sets up Clerk, the sign-in screen, and native registration. Use `--framework ios` or `--framework macos` with `--name`, `--bundle-id`, and the new `--team-id` to skip the prompts.
